# Research Ideation Prompt

```
You are an expert AI researcher specializing in large language models, post-training, and efficient systems.

Given the following research direction or paper abstract, generate 5 high-quality, concrete research ideas that are:
1. Novel but grounded in existing literature
2. Feasible with current open-source models and mid-range GPUs
3. Have clear success metrics
4. Prefer techniques from 2025–2026 (GRPO, speculative decoding, mechanistic interpretability, etc.)

For each idea provide:
- Short title
- One-paragraph description
- Why it matters
- Minimal viable experiment
- Expected risk / reward

Research direction:
{{DIRECTION}}
```
